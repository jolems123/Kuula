# Guarantor identity and pledged collateral evidence

- Guarantors use their **own** authenticated Kuula account to submit National ID front/back and portrait. An independently verified KYC record containing all three images is required before viewing guarantee terms, requesting a signature code, or signing.
- Guarantee signatures bind to the approved KYC submission ID; the credit approver rechecks the evidence reference. Evidence is not collected from borrowers on behalf of a guarantor.
- Borrowers can optionally photograph pledged loan security/collateral during application. The image is stored in the existing restricted field-evidence storage with per-application authorization. A photo is not proof of legal title, valid charge/perfection, or valuation.
- Both flows need privacy notice, retention configuration, staff access review, identity/document review, and real-device tests before live financial release.
- The existing legal approval gate remains mandatory. Security image controls do not replace consent, lawful processing, or enforceability review.
