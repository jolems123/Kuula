More about Kuula business loans

From application to Mobile Money

Kuula does not promise instant approval. A credit officer looks at your business, and you see the full cost and the agreement before any money moves.

Read the whole process

  1. Create your account

    Verify your phone number and your National ID.

  2. Tell us about your business

    What you sell, where you trade, and your monthly sales and costs.

  3. Get reviewed and receive an offer

    Our credit team checks your application and may visit your business.

  4. Accept and get paid

    Read and accept the agreement; the money is sent to your Mobile Money.

Lending that fits a business, not a payslip.

Kuula looks at what your business earns after its costs, your trading history and what you already owe. Your first loan is sized carefully, and your limit grows as you repay on time.

What we will never do

  • Charge fees that are not in your offer.
  • Send money before you accept the agreement.
  • Ask for your PIN by phone, SMS or email.
