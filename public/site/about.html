About Kuula

Kuula Microfinance Limited provides business loans to traders, shop owners and growing businesses in Uganda.

Why Kuula exists

Most of Uganda's businesses are small: a stall, a shop, a workshop, a salon. They often need a little capital at the right moment to restock or take on an order, and too often the only options are expensive, unclear or hard to reach.

Kuula offers a business loan you can apply for on your phone, at a price written down before you accept, with the money paid to the Mobile Money account you already use.

How we lend

  • Business loans only, for a business purpose.
  • One clear rate and no hidden fees.
  • Every application reviewed by people, with independent approval.
  • Loans sized to what the business can repay.
  • Identity checked to protect customers from fraud.

Your information

We collect what we need to verify your identity and assess your application, keep identity photos in encrypted storage, and limit who on our team can see them. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Responsible borrowing

Borrow only what your business needs and can repay. If you expect to have trouble repaying, contact us early: it is always easier to find a way forward before a payment is missed.

Talk to us or apply

Have a question about your business and a Kuula loan? Our team is happy to help.
