Pricing

One rate, no fees, and the full cost in writing before you accept.

33.6% a year

Simple interest, charged only on the amount you borrow and only for the days in your loan term. There are no application, processing or disbursement fees.

Interest is worked out as: amount borrowed × 33.6% × days ÷ 365, rounded to the nearest shilling.

What is not charged

  • No application fee.
  • No processing or disbursement fee.
  • No fees beyond the interest shown in your offer.

Your Mobile Money provider may charge its own transaction fees under its tariffs.

Repaying

Repay through Mobile Money from the app, in part or in full, at any time before your due date. Each payment is applied first to interest and then to the amount borrowed, and you get a receipt once it is confirmed.

Before you accept

Your offer states the amount, interest, total to repay and due date, and the loan agreement sets out your rights and obligations. Read both. If anything is unclear, ask us before you accept.
