Who can apply

Your business story matters.

Assessment considers whether your business can sustainably repay. No loan is approved based solely on registering an account.

  • Currently running a business with a defined trading activity
  • Prepared to explain its products, customers, location and operating history
  • Able to provide business income, expenses and existing debt details
  • Applying for a productive business purpose with a realistic repayment plan
  • Able to complete identity and business verification requirements
Responsible borrowing comes first.

Loans and offered durations depend on affordability and eligibility. Kuula may request supporting documents or additional verification. Review the full cost of credit, due dates and agreement before you accept.

Some shorter loan terms may be restricted by app-store rules or applicable regulations and will only be offered where permitted.

Common questions

Questions before applying?

Here is what to expect from a business-focused lending service.

Can I apply without a registered company?

Kuula is designed to consider existing informal and registered businesses. All applications still require eligibility, identity and business verification.

How long can I borrow for?

Business loan terms are designed around 30–365 days, subject to the product, affordability, legal requirements and distribution-channel restrictions. Your actual offer may differ.

Will my application be approved immediately?

No. Applications go through field verification, credit review and a final decision. Approval is never guaranteed.

Will I be able to review the agreement?

Approved applicants should review the exact offer and agreement before acceptance. Funds are only released after separate disbursement controls are met.

Does Kuula offer savings or personal loans?

No. Kuula's customer-facing focus is lending for existing businesses and productive business uses.
